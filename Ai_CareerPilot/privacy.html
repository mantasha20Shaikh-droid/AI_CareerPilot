Privacy policy

This portfolio application stores account details, career profile information, and resume analysis results in its configured database. Uploaded resumes are processed temporarily and removed after analysis. Do not upload sensitive documents to a demo deployment.

Local development data stays on the machine unless you configure external services. If an external AI provider is configured, review its data handling terms before sending resume content.

This starter policy is not legal advice and should be reviewed before public deployment.
